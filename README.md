Study Description
This usability study compares two ride-hailing applications, Waymo and Uber. Although both applications allow users to request transportation, their interfaces differ in how users enter destinations, view ride information, and manage trips.
The purpose of this study is to determine which application provides a more intuitive and user-friendly experience when performing common ride-booking tasks.
Participants will be asked to complete the same three tasks using both applications. The tasks focus on requesting a ride, locating important trip information, and modifying a trip before booking.
Task 1: Request a Ride to a Specific Destination
Imagine you are at Union Station (800 N Alameda St, Los Angeles, CA 90012) and want to travel to The Broad museum (221 S Grand Ave, Los Angeles, CA 90012).
Using the application, enter Union Station as your pickup location and The Broad as your destination. Navigate through the booking process until you reach the final step before requesting the ride. Do not confirm the booking.
This task evaluates how easily users can navigate the application, enter trip information, and complete the initial steps of requesting a ride.
Task 2: Find the Estimated Ride Price and Pickup Time
You are planning a ride from Union Station (800 N Alameda St, Los Angeles, CA 90012) to The Broad museum (221 S Grand Ave, Los Angeles, CA 90012).
Using the application, find the estimated cost of the ride and the estimated pickup time or vehicle arrival time. Report both values to the researcher without booking the ride.
This task evaluates how easily users can locate and understand important trip information before making a booking decision.
Task 3: Change a Ride Destination Before Booking
You are planning a ride from Union Station (800 N Alameda St, Los Angeles, CA 90012) to The Broad museum (221 S Grand Ave, Los Angeles, CA 90012). However, before booking, you decide to go to Walt Disney Concert Hall (111 S Grand Ave, Los Angeles, CA 90012) instead.
Using the application, change your destination from The Broad to Walt Disney Concert Hall while keeping the same pickup location. Continue until the application displays the updated trip information. Do not confirm the booking.
This task evaluates how easily users can modify trip details, navigate between screens, and recover from a change in plans.
